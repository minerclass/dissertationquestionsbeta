What to expect

The interview pages display the current question and card-sort wording for committee and IRB review. They do not accept participant responses.

Current review-only state

  • Response entry, browser recording, local saving, and file export are disabled.
  • The pages do not send participant information to GitHub Pages or another service.
  • The shared ten-card 05F sort appears only on the 05A and 05C review pages.
  • The 05B school/system-leader page contains no card sort.
  • Any later activation must match the written IRB-approved protocol and data-management process.

Protocol boundaries

  • No recruitment or participant activity begins before written IRB approval and other applicable permissions.
  • The card sort is a separate optional activity for eligible 05A and 05C participants.
  • Participants may skip any interview question or stop participation under the approved consent process.
  • There are no correct responses; the study is interested in lived experience and professional judgment.
