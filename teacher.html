Classroom-Facing Educator Instrument
Estimated session length: 60–75 minutes in total (50–65-minute core interview plus an optional approximately 10-minute card sort)

Consent and Context

This read-only page displays the draft participant-facing instrument for review. It does not collect or export responses.

Standard opening script: Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. This conversation is part of a study on how educators and school-system leaders make sense of generative AI in relation to teaching and learning. There are no right or wrong answers; I am interested in your honest experience, including where you disagree with how others frame these issues. Your participation is voluntary, you may skip any question, and you may stop at any time. With your permission I will make an audio recording of the interview to support accurate transcription and analysis; you may decline recording. Do you have any questions before we begin? Do I have your permission to record?
Research caution: teachers may skip any question. Avoid collecting names, school names, or identifiable student information in open responses.
Recording scope: this page does not record browser audio or video. If the participant gives written permission for interview recording, use the separate IRB-approved audio recording process. The participant may decline recording and still take part.
All response data remains local until export.

Interview Questions

The researcher may enter notes or a reviewed transcript after approval. Browser-local recording is not available for this protocol.

Teacher Card Sort

This separate optional card sort matches working attachment 05F. In live administration, shuffle the cards and ask the participant to place each one on both dimensions while thinking aloud. Participants may revise placements at any time.

Neutral follow-ups, used selectively: What did you observe and what did you infer? What additional context would change this placement? What should remain the learner's responsibility? What evidence would help you judge the result?
